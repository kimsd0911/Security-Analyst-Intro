<This exercise compares my past 24/7 enterprise monitoring experience in South Korea with North American SOC frameworks to bridge operational gaps for a SOC Analyst role in Canada.>
 

Team Shift Structure: Four analysts maintained continuous 24/7 monitoring coverage using a four-team, two-shift day and night schedule.

Organizational Hierarchy: Unlike specialized Western SOC tiers, there were no separate higher-tier analysts, SOC Engineers, Penetration Testers, or CERT Leads. The team reported directly to a SOC Leader and a Manager.

Escalation and On-Site Coordination: Analysts oversaw both the monitoring center and the server room. For cross-departmental, regional, or international server alerts, we escalated incidents to the Manager to coordinate resolution with external teams. For local server room hardware failures, we contacted the responsible engineers directly and facilitated physical access for on-site repairs.

--> While slight differences may exist depending on a company's size and industry, the underlying operational principles remain the same even if the specific tool names differ. Nevertheless, to build hands-on familiarity with industry-standard software, I practiced using widely adopted tools such as SIEM, Splunk and Wireshark in subsequent labs.
