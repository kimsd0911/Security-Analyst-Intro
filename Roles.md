🔍 Operational Comparison: South Korea vs. North American SOC
Objective: This exercise compares my past 24/7 enterprise monitoring experience in South Korea with North American SOC frameworks to bridge operational gaps for a SOC Analyst role in Canada.

🕒 Team Shift Structure: Four analysts maintained continuous 24/7 monitoring coverage using a four-team, two-shift day and night schedule.

🏢 Organizational Hierarchy: Unlike specialized Western SOC tiers, there were no separate higher-tier analysts, SOC Engineers, Penetration Testers, or CERT Leads. The team reported directly to a SOC Leader and a Manager.

📞 Escalation & On-Site Coordination: Analysts oversaw both the monitoring center and the server room.

Remote/Cross-Departmental Alerts: Escalated incidents to the Manager to coordinate resolution with external, regional, or international teams.

Local Hardware Failures: Contacted responsible engineers directly and facilitated physical access to the server room for on-site repairs.

💡 Key Takeaway & Tool Adaptation
Transferable Core Principles: While slight differences may exist depending on a company's size and industry, the underlying operational principles remain the same even if the specific tool names differ.

Hands-On Lab Practice: To build practical familiarity with industry-standard software, I actively practiced using widely adopted tools such as SIEM, Splunk, and Wireshark in subsequent labs.
